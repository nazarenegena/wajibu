# Project Wajibu — Demo Video Script

**Target length: 90 seconds**
**Format: screen recording + short talking-head intro/outro**

---

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook
**Visual:** A real Nyeri tender notice on screen, jargon highlighted in red.
**Voiceover:**
> "In Nyeri, tenders are public. But for most people, they might as well be written in another language."

---

## 0:10–0:25 — Problem
**Visual:** Scroll through the PDF — legal terms, deadlines buried on page 14.
**Voiceover:**
> "Technical English. Procurement jargon. Deadlines and eligibility rules hidden deep in the document. If you can't understand it, you can't bid. You can't question it. You're shut out."

---

## 0:25–0:40 — Solution intro
**Visual:** Wajibu landing page.
**Voiceover:**
> "This is Project Wajibu. It takes a real county tender and explains it in plain Swahili or English — in seconds."

---

## 0:40–1:05 — Live demo
**Visual:** Screen recording, no cuts:
1. Select the NYEWASCO tender from samples
2. Summary appears in English
3. Toggle to Swahili — text switches
4. Tap "Framework Contract" → plain explanation pops up
5. Red flag appears: short deadline, with source quote

**Voiceover:**
> "Every claim cites the source. Every technical term is explained. Every red flag points back to the exact line in the document."

---

## 1:05–1:20 — Next steps
**Visual:** Next steps screen — "Ask your MCA", "Report to PPRA", "Learn more".
**Voiceover:**
> "And every result ends with a clear next step: the question to ask, and who to ask. Because understanding is only useful if it leads to action."

---

## 1:20–1:30 — Close
**Visual:** Wajibu logo + tagline.
**Voiceover:**
> "Project Wajibu. Because public money should be publicly understood."

---

## Recording Tips
- Record at 1080p, 30fps
- Use a clean browser window (no bookmarks bar, no other tabs)
- Pre-load the sample documents so upload is instant
- Speak slowly; the demo is doing the work
- Keep total under 90 seconds — judges watch many
